# Data Structures & Algorithms Interview Study Guide

A practical interview-preparation guide paired with the [Algorithm Practice IntelliJ plugin](../README.md). Use these notes to build a repeatable 45-minute problem-solving process, recognize algorithmic patterns from problem constraints, and sequence your practice across the plugin's study guides and tracks.

## Contents

1. **[Coding Interview Playbook](interview-playbook.md)** — 45-minute round pacing, clarifying questions, brute-force-to-optimal framing, dry-running without an IDE, and edge-case checklists.
2. **[Pattern Recognition & Complexity Cheat Sheet](pattern-and-complexity-cheat-sheet.md)** — input-size `N` to target Big-O heuristics, signal-to-pattern mapping aligned with the plugin's 18 study guides, data structure operation costs, and Java interview idioms and pitfalls.
3. **[4-Week & 8-Week Study Plans](study-plan.md)** — structured schedules combining the plugin's 18 technique guides (`src/main/resources/guides/index.txt`), curated problem tracks (`blind-75`, `neetcode-150`, and company tracks in `src/main/resources/tracks/index.txt`), spaced repetition, and timed mock rounds.

## How to use this guide with the plugin

- **Learn one technique at a time:** Read the technique overview in the plugin's **Study Guides** view (or scan the [Pattern Cheat Sheet](pattern-and-complexity-cheat-sheet.md)), then solve 2–3 Easy/Medium problems from that guide without peeking at hints.
- **Practice under realistic constraints:** Timebox Medium problems to 25 minutes and Hard problems to 40 minutes. Write your own edge cases and trace through them by hand *before* clicking **Run Examples** or **Check Solution**.
- **Review deliberately:** After passing (or getting stuck past 25 minutes), use the plugin's **Check Solution** complexity probe and optional **AI Coach** (*Diagnose Failure*, *Compare Approaches*, *Follow-Up*) to verify asymptotic optimality and articulate trade-offs out loud.
- **Close the loop with spaced repetition:** Any problem you needed a hint for or failed on the first check goes onto a retry list to re-solve cold 3 days and 14 days later.
